OVERVIEW

"Dreams shaped me and made me know what is real and what is unreal." The project highlights the value of understanding dreams as a powerful method for self-exploration, ultimately fostering a mindset of self-enquiry. Designed with InDesign, Photoshop, and custom typography.

This is Harry's MFA thesis book, exploring the profound phenomenon of lucid dreaming. Through interactive design pieces and art installations, Harry guides readers to understand lucid dreaming by simulating the sensation of conscious dreaming.

THESIS BOOK

Dream Reality

Dream Reality is Harry's MFA graduation thesis book. Through interactive design and art installations, it transforms the concept of lucid dreaming into an experience you can feel. You are warmly invited to open its pages and explore the boundary between dreams and reality.
